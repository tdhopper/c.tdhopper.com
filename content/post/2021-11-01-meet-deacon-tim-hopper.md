David Nakhla interviewed me for the first episode of [*The Reformed Deacon*](https://www.thereformeddeacon.org/1849391/episodes/9472090-meet-deacon-tim-hopper). We talked about my upbringing and church background, how I became a deacon, the origins of [ReformedDeacon.com](https://reformeddeacon.com/), and my republication of John Lorimer's *The Deaconship*.
